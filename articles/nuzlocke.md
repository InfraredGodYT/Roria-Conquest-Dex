# Nuzlocke

A nuzlocke is a Pokémon challenge of completing a playthrough with specific self-imposed rules to make the game harder. These rules include the following:

- You can only catch the first Pokémon you encounter in every location (unless you've caught it before).
- If your Pokémon faints, it's dead and completely unusable.
- You must nickname all of your Pokémon.
- You can't level up past your current level cap (see below).

### What are Level Caps?
Level caps prevent you from overleveling your Pokémon before the next Gym Leader. They only apply to Nuzlocke gamemodes and are in place to ensure you make it through the Nuzlocke challenge with skill and nothing more.

Your level cap will scale to how many Gym Badges you have and affects any source of EXP (Battle, Rare Candies, Breeding, etc).

### Nuzlocke Hall Of Fame

In order to be included in the Roria Conquest [Nuzlocke Hall Of Fame](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/11nPWZSpdCaMkZxmVRiE_N9827sHwvF_JiC2hUHv4wDU/edit?usp=sharing), you must also abide by the following **additional rules:**

- Battle Mode must be **"Set"** in Options.
- Any main boss fights **must be recorded.** *(Includes Gym Leaders, E4, Rivals, Evil Team Admins/Leaders, etc.)*
- **No healing** during a battle.
- Fast Forward must be **turned off** during any recorded fights.
- Looking at a Raid Den will **count as an encounter**.
