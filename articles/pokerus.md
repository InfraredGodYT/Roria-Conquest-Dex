# Pokerus

This article explains how Pokerus works in Roria Conquest. Keep in mind that this is VERY different from [vanilla Pokerus](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9rus).

### Acquisition

You have a **1/512** chance of 1 Pokemon in your party getting infected after each battle. If it stays in your party, there's a 1/3 chance the Pokerus will spread from its holder to one of the adjacent Pokemon in your party after each battle. Pokemon that are already
infected or cured cannot get the Pokerus again. Nothing in-game currently exists to boost these odds (3/17/2026).

### Strains and Becoming Cured

Anytime a Pokemon is infected with Pokerus, it'll have a strain level between 1 and 4, determining the **experience boost** and the
**number of days** it will stay infected before its cured of the virus. The number of days a Pokemon will stay infected is the exact same number as the strain level of Pokerus it has, and the experience boost is 25% multiplied by the strain level.

In your infected Pokemon's summary, the color and presence of crossed lines on the Pokerus icon indicate the infection status and strain level. The absence of said crossed lines means the Pokemon has been cured.

| Color   | Strain |
|--------:|:------:|
| Pink    | 1      |
| Purple  | 2      |
| Red     | 3      |
| Black   | 4      |


### Experience Boost Formula

Final XP = (1 + (strain level / 4)) x Base XP

Stacks with any other experience boosts in-game, including (but not limited to) the daily bonus and Ro-Powers.

### Effects

- 2x EVs from Battles
- EXP Boost (1.25x - 2x depending on strain level)
- 2x Happiness
- 2x Faster Egg Steps when Breeding (if either Parent has Pokerus)
- 1 more inherited IV from Breeding (if either Parent has Pokerus)
